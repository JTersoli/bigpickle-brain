# Example Agency

Replace this note with your own. Everything in this folder goes into the agents' system prompt, so keep it to what they need: who you are, who you work with, what you sell and for how much. Add `private: true` to the frontmatter of any note that must never be sent to the model.

## Who we are

A small digital agency for local businesses: cafés, bakeries, bars, restaurants and food brands. Direct tone, no jargon. We reply within a business day.

## Who we work with

- **Small businesses** without a website, or with one they are ashamed of. They sell through Instagram or WhatsApp and lose orders on the way.
- **Growing businesses** with a site and some ads, but nothing connected: no analytics, no follow-up, no automation.

## Services and prices (USD, excluding tax)

| Service | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | One page, mobile first, basic SEO, contact form | 500 – 700 |
| Business website | Up to 5 pages, menu or services, map, contact | 1,000 – 1,800 |
| Online ordering | Catalogue, cart, checkout, order notifications by WhatsApp | 2,500 – 4,500 |
| AI assistant on the site or WhatsApp | Answers questions and takes bookings 24/7 | 400 – 900 |
| Monthly care | Hosting, updates, backups, small changes | 60 – 150 per month |

Anything outside these ranges is quoted after a call.

## Terms

- 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Two rounds of changes included.
- The client owns the domain, the accounts and the code.
