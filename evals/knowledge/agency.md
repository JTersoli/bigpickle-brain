# Northside Digital

A fictional agency, used only by the evals. Everything here is made up.

## Who we are

A small digital agency in Sydney for local food businesses: cafés, bakeries, restaurants and bars. Direct tone, short sentences, no hype. We never promise rankings, sales figures or follower counts. We reply within a business day.

## Who we work with

- **Small businesses** without a website, or with one they are ashamed of. They sell through Instagram or WhatsApp and lose orders on the way.
- **Growing businesses** with a site and some ads, but nothing connected: no analytics, no follow-up, no automation.

## Services and prices (AUD, excluding GST)

| Service | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | One page, mobile first, basic SEO, contact form | $800 – $1,200 |
| Business website | Up to 5 pages, menu or services, map, contact form | $1,500 – $2,500 |
| Online ordering | Catalogue, cart, checkout, order notifications | $3,000 – $5,000 |
| Google Business Profile setup | Listing, photos, hours, categories, first posts | $300 – $500 |
| AI assistant on the site or WhatsApp | Answers questions and takes bookings 24/7 | $600 – $1,000 |
| Monthly care | Hosting, updates, backups, small changes | $80 – $150 per month |

Anything else is quoted after a call: set its price to 0 and say so in the price note.

## Terms

- 50% to start, 50% on delivery. Two rounds of changes included.
- The client owns the domain, the accounts and the code.
- Ad spend, platform fees and payment-provider fees are paid by the client.
- No guarantees of rankings, traffic or sales. No free trials.
- Timelines, from the kickoff: a landing page takes 1 to 2 weeks, a business website 3 to 4 weeks, online ordering 5 to 7 weeks.
